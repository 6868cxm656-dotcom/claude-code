How our risk process works

1
Continuous

Dynamic updates

Risk owners update their risks whenever something changes — new risks, rescores, control updates. The register is live, not a quarterly snapshot.

2
Quarterly

SLT collective review

The SLT reviews the full register together each quarter: scores, appetite breaches, overdue actions and anything new.

3
Before each subcommittee

Owner pre-review

Ahead of each Board subcommittee meeting, the relevant SLT owners review and refresh the risks in their areas.

4
Each subcommittee meeting

Subcommittee scrutiny

Each Board subcommittee reviews the risks relevant to its remit, using the register and the change report since it last met.

5
Each Board meeting

Full Board review

The Board reviews the risk register in full at each meeting, with the change report showing what has moved and why.

Biggest organisational priorities — Q3 2026 (July–September) AI-drafted — for SLT to challenge

This analysis was drafted by Claude (AI) from the consolidated register of 11 June 2026 — the legacy register merged with the new taxonomy — focusing on the risks sitting furthest outside draft appetite, and TCF's known plans (portal build, office move, Activate, the autumn risk-management overhaul). It is a static starting point for SLT discussion, not a live calculation: it does not update as the register changes, and the SLT should re-derive priorities at each quarterly review.

1 · Reputation readiness on identity and legacy

Public criticism of TCF's identity and positioning (R11, scoring 16) is the highest residual risk on the register — carried over from the legacy register at 4×4 — and sits well above its Balanced: averse tolerance (≤9), with media mishandling (R13, 9) right at the line.

  • Agree refreshed positioning lines on founder legacy and environment by 30 Sep · Jacqui & Julia
  • Run a crisis communications simulation with SLT by 31 Oct · Jacqui
  • Confirm whether 16 is still the right residual score — or what new mitigation would move it Sep SLT · Jacqui

2 · Cyber, data and continuity resilience before the portal lands

Cyber (R36, 12) and data protection (R37, 8) sit far above their Averse tolerance (≤4), and business continuity (R38, 12) is outside ≤9 — just as the portal (R33, 12) widens the attack surface and the office move changes the continuity picture.

  • Test incident response and a full restore from backup by 31 Jul · Jim
  • Complete DPIAs for the portal and AI tools before go-live by 31 Aug · Jim
  • Refresh and test the BCP for the new office by 30 Sep · Jim

3 · Safety-critical assurance for Fellows and staff

Fellow safety overseas (R07, 9), safeguarding (R08, 6) and threats against TCF people (R27, 9) are all above their Averse tolerance (≤4) — these are the risks the framework says are always managed with full aversion, with peak Fellowship travel ahead.

  • Run the live test of the overseas emergency response protocol by 31 Jul · Nikesh
  • Complete the safeguarding audit and refresher training by 31 Jul · Nikesh
  • Review hostile-actor monitoring and escalation with building security by 30 Sep · Julia

4 · Financial control and income resilience

Fraud and internal controls (R25, 8) and donor due diligence (R17, 6) sit above Averse tolerance (≤4), while income shortfall and concentration (R16, 9) is at the edge of its line in a difficult funding environment.

  • Fraud awareness training incl. invoice fraud scenarios by 31 Aug · Jim
  • Refresh due diligence thresholds for major gifts by 31 Aug · Chris
  • Agree income concentration limits and diversification targets by 30 Sep · Chris

5 · Protect capacity and land the new framework

Stress and change fatigue (R26, 9 against ≤4) coincide with Q3 stacking the office move, portal and risk overhaul on the same team (R32). And this register only works if owners confirm the consolidated risks — especially the DRAFT-tagged scores and the generic risks added from the taxonomy.

  • Each owner confirms titles, scores and committee allocations in their areas by 31 Jul · all SLT
  • Capacity-check and sequence the autumn change calendar by 15 Sep · Jim & Julia
  • First full quarterly SLT review using the change report; taxonomy to ARGC and Board by 30 Sep · Julia

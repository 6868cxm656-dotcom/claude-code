# TCF Risk Taxonomy — DRAFT v3

*For SLT discussion. Extracted from `TCF_Risk_Taxonomy__DRAFT_v3.pptx`.*

## Purpose

One shared map of TCF's risks, with a named SLT owner for every area, so we manage risk deliberately instead of reactively. This will enable us to better communicate with the team and the Board about risk, and will be a prelude to an overhaul of our risk register this autumn.

- **A common language** — One set of categories everyone uses: SLT, Board, team plans and the risk register.
- **A complete picture** — The taxonomy is a checklist for blind spots. If a category has no risks logged against it, we should be asking why.
- **Clear ownership** — Every Level 1 category has one named SLT owner. They don't carry every risk themselves, but they make sure their area is assessed, controlled and reported.
- **Proportionate effort** — With a draft appetite against each Level 2 risk area, we put control and assurance effort where we genuinely want less risk, and free things up where we are willing to embrace opportunities.

**What this is not.** It isn't a risk register: the register lists actual risks, scores them and tracks actions. The taxonomy is the foundation the register sits on. And it isn't final: appetite is owned and set by the Board, so everything here is a proposal to shape that conversation.

**What happens next.** SLT confirms categories, owners and draft appetite, then the draft taxonomy goes to ARG and Board for feedback. Following that feedback, we'll undertake a wider overhaul of risk management in autumn 2026: rebuilding the risk register around this map, drafting the appetite statement for Board approval, resetting how risk is reported and reviewed, and launching this with the Leadership team and wider team.

## Appetite scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Averse** | Minimise or eliminate risk wherever practicable |
| **Balanced: averse** | Minimise risk without chasing elimination |
| **Balanced: seeking** | Accept calculated risk proportionate to the opportunity |
| **Seeking** | Accept risk in pursuit of bigger returns |

> Whatever the setting, legal and regulatory compliance, safeguarding, and the safety of staff and Fellows are always managed with full risk aversion.
